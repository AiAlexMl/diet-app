# Macro & Menu Algorithm

## Diet/allergy filter (`allowed`)

Excludes by avoided-set, allergy tags, and diet: vegan/vegetarian/lactose; **`gluten_free` excludes `gluten`-tagged foods**; `gfOnly` items appear only for GF users; `supplement`/`vegOnly`/`containsMilk`/`optIn` gating as documented in `data-schema.md`.

## Macro Calculation (`calcMacro`)

- **BMR**: Harris-Benedict 1919
- **RMR** = BMR × activity factor: **1.2** for cut / not training / no training time chosen yet, **1.375** for a trained maintain or bulk (so maintenance is accurate and the bulk surplus is real)
- **Calorie target**:
  - cut: RMR − `min(500, rmr×0.20)` (dynamic deficit, max 20% of RMR)
  - maintain: RMR
  - bulk: RMR + 300
  - Floor: `Math.max(target, female ? 1200 : 1500)`
- **Protein**: `weight × 2g` (**vegans: × 1.6g** — hard to reach 2 from plants); if BMI ≥ 30, uses lean-body proxy (`25 × height_m²`) instead
- **Fat target**: `max(25g men / 40g women, 25% of target ÷ 9)` — 25% of calories (raised from 20% on 12/07/2026 per a coach's expert feedback: 20% is the low end, WHO caps at ≤30%; 25% improves satiety without starving carbs). Gram floors unchanged. Measured (2016-build matrix): fat lands at 25.0% median, calorie accuracy *improved* (overall dev −1.5%→−0.8%), protein unchanged, fiber <14g/1000 nudged 2.4%→3.0% (fewer carbs ⇒ slightly less fiber; still well under the pre-fiber-work 7.3%). Carbs stay abundant by goal: cut ~43% / maintain ~52% / bulk ~57%. **Bonus:** less carb budget ⇒ **fewer inflated carb piles** — hot meals with ≥300g carbs dropped 57%→40.5% and carb-pile splits per menu 0.52→0.32 (the split mechanism below fires less because the underlying pile shrank).
- **Carb floor**: if `protein×4 + fat×9 + 400 > target`, target is raised to fit — sets `S.carbWarning`
- **Carbs**: `(target − protein×4 − fat×9) / 4`

## State Flags

- `S.bmiWarning` — set by `buildMenu()`: cut+BMI<20 or bulk+BMI≥30
- `S.carbWarning` — set by `calcMacro()`: when target was raised to meet macro floor

## Menu Building (`buildMenu`)

1. Calls `calcMacro()`
2. Sets `S.bmiWarning` (cut+BMI<20 or bulk+BMI≥30)
3. **All four `MEAL_TIMES` schedules hold 4 meals.** `morning` was the lone 3-meal schedule until 06/08/2026, which also left it the longest gap in the app (13:00→20:00, seven hours) while the *untrained* day had a 17:30 snack. Below ~2,100 kcal — where `mealPlan` adds nothing — its biggest plate carried **39–41 % of the day**. Adding an אחה"צ snack at 16:30 (`.30/.25/.13/.32`) measured, over 600 menus per variant: biggest plate **40.8 %→34.1 %** (cut) and **38.9 %→33.5 %** (maintain), carbs on that plate 117 g→108 g, **calorie accuracy and protein/kg unchanged**, and total `S.menuWarning` count across the 1,440-run matrix **46→36**. The feared tiny portions did not appear: even under 1,400 kcal the smallest meal stays ~172 kcal (a yogurt and fruit), so **no calorie threshold gates it**.
4. `mealPlan(key, target)` picks the `MEAL_TIMES` schedule (by `S.time`/`S.noTrain`) and **appends extra snacks for high calorie targets** (>2100 → +1, >2400 → +2, >2900 → +3), each at the **midpoint of the schedule's largest gap** (`snackSlot`, rounded to 15 min, gap ≥ 2 h, never the pre→post-workout gap — that is the workout). ⚠️ Until 11/11/2026 the times were fixed (10:30/16:00/21:30/14:30) and collided with the schedule: "אחרי אימון 16:00" + "נשנוש נוסף 16:00", dinner 21:00 + snack 21:30 — 168 of 1,344 menus. Re-sorted by time and `pct` re-normalised — so a bulk's calories spread over 5–7 meals instead of ballooning single portions. **Single-hot-meal schedules (`noTrain`/`morning`) with target > 2200 get one extra snack** so the lone hot meal doesn't carry ~50 % of the day (acute on restrictive diets where the other meals can't fill).
5. **Budget-aware order**: builds the **non-hot** meals first (mostly unit portions), then the **hot** meal(s) last with `budget = target − Σ(other meals' cal)` (split by `pct` if two hot meals) — so the gram-weighted meat+carb fill the gap from the start. Display order is the original schedule order.
6. Each meal is built via `buildMealBest(type, budget, used, ctx)` — runs `buildMeal` 5× (random templates), each on a **cloned** `used` Map, and keeps the best by score = calorie-fit **+ a lean-fat preference** (penalises fat above the meal's expected share `S.fatG·budget/target`) **+ a fiber preference** (weight 0.7 penalty below the meal's expected fiber share `S.fibG·budget/target` = the floor; weight 0.5 penalty above **2.5×** that share = a far-band ceiling that reins in vegan legume piles without touching normal menus). This "swap a meal that fits better" both hits the budget and steers toward lean foods (chicken breast over thigh, 0% yogurt, white over yellow cheese) so fat stays near target **from the source** — the biggest fat lever, since most fat comes from fixed-portion protein foods. Fiber uses the same from-the-source principle: **no reconcile stage** (fiber rides on food *choice*, not grams — a Stage 4 would fight Stages 1–3). Shares the `used` Map and `ctx.usedCarbCats`.
   **Fiber target**: `S.fibG = round(target × 14/1000)` in `calcMacro` (IOM: 14 g/1000 kcal). **Measured (07/2026, 2016-build matrix)**: builds <14 g/1000 kcal dropped 7.3% → **2.4%** (excluding the structurally carb-poor gluten-free segment: ~1.1%; GF stays ~10% — no whole breads/oats); vegan daily fiber (structural excess — plant-protein targets force legume mass) 75.8 → **66.3 g** median vs a 25–38 g recommendation — residual excess accepted & now visible in the UI; a user-facing warning banner was deliberately deferred. Calorie/protein/fat accuracy unchanged (calorie medians ≤±2.8% in every segment, vegan protein 1.5 g/kg, fat p90 ×1.1). Related: `isProt` (Stage 1) counts a legume as a protein lever **only if `p ≥ 7`** (consistent with `convertDemeatedMeal`) — peas (p 5.4) was being inflated to 350 g for protein, dragging fiber; it can still appear at template-picked size (~300 g vegan mains — residual).
7. After all meals: if no fruit was used and `target > 1200`, injects one fruit into the snack
8. **`reconcile(meals)`** (runs last) — adjusts the menu so the daily macros land near target

## Macro reconciliation (`reconcile`) — 3 stages

The build is bottom-up (per-item rounding, clamps, skipped `optional` slots), so raw totals drift. `reconcile(meals)` runs an outer loop (≤6) of three stages so protein & fat land near target and carbs absorb the rest of the calories.

**Main-protein realism floor + concentration (pre-loop, before Stage 1).** A cooked **meat/fish main** (slot with `protPct`, tagged `_mainProt` + `_minG` in `buildFromTemplate`) must be a realistic portion — `mainProtFloor()` goal-aware **cut 70 / maintain 85 / bulk 90 g** (prevents the old ~30 g meat for a low-weight bulker). Pre-loop pass: the meat main kept (raised to its floor) is chosen by **workout-adjacency first** (a meal tagged `pre`/`post`), then by grams — so when concentrating, the surviving meat sits on the training meal, not an untagged lunch (matters for the evening schedule, which has both an untagged 12:30 hot meal and a `pre` 17:30 one; nutrient timing is secondary to total protein per ISSN, but if we keep only one meat meal it should be the workout one). An **additional** meat meal that would push total protein **> `S.proteinG×1.15`** has its meat **removed** — i.e., a low-protein-budget day concentrates to **one** meat meal instead of inflating protein. To avoid a bare carb+salad meal, the de-meated meal is rebuilt by **`convertDemeatedMeal`** (omnivores only — vegans/vegetarians never concentrate): it keeps the existing carb (the day's flexible calorie lever) and **adds a light protein anchor by weighted lottery** — egg (w3) / cottage-yogurt (w2) / legume (w1, high-protein only `p≥7` so no peas, given a `_minG≈120 g` floor so Stage 1 can't shrink it to a token portion). When the anchor is a legume the meal's carb is **capped per-item** (`_maxG`: grain ≤250 g / unit-starch ≤2 units) so a legume meal is "legume + one moderate carb + veg" — never a 3-carb pile; the overflow calories flow to the meat meal. `clampG` respects `it._minG` so Stages 1/post-loop never shrink a floored main below it. Carbs (Stage 3) absorb the calorie shift. **Carb-pile split (post-loop):** in any hot meal **without a legume** the single heaviest flexible carb >~350 kcal (grain *or* unit-starch) is split 1:1 into two components of **different** types (grain→adds sweet-potato/potato; starch→adds grain), so no plate is a lone 450 g rice or 3-sweet-potato pile (≤2 carb items/meal). `_maxG` is honored by `reUnit` and Stage 3's `maxOf`/grain-cap. Verified (450-build fuzz): 0 meat <60 g, protein/kg ≤~2.5 even at the extreme (48 kg bulk), normal weight unaffected. Vegan/vegetarian legume mains are not floored. Tolerances: `PROT_TOL=0.07`, `FAT_TOL=0.08`, `CAL_TOL=0.04`. Per-item caps: generic protein ≤350 g (`clampG`), **meat/fish mains ≤ `mainProtCap()` — cut 250 / maintain 280 / bulk 320 g** (a real plate, not the old 350; enforced in `clampG`, the pre-loop mains pass, and `adjustFat`'s lean-swap — the protein remainder flows to Stage 1b's top-up injection), grams-elastic grain via **`grainCap(f)`** — goal-aware so big portions are realistic. Schedule-aware: **single-hot-meal days** (`singleHotMeal()` = `noTrain` or not noon/evening) use higher caps **cut 320 / maintain 480 / bulk 600 g** (the lone hot meal is the calorie sink; the carb-split keeps the plate realistic), while **multi-hot (noon/evening)** keep cut 280 / maintain 350 / bulk 450 g — unchanged. Oats (`breakfast` tag, ids 41/106) tighter at cut 280 / maintain 300 / bulk 350 (a breakfast isn't the day's calorie sink).

**Undershoot handling (top-up, ALL schedules — upgraded 06/07/2026):** Stage 3's proportional-by-calorie distribution under-fills when most levers are capped unit-foods, so a **post-loop top-up** runs in three tiers, each only while still > `CAL_TOL` under target: **(a)** grow existing elastic grains to `grainCap`; **(b)** grow count-levers unit-by-unit (bread→2, crackers→gram-cap, unit-starch→3, or **→2 when the meal has another carb item** — keeps plates un-piled); **(c)** **inject an elastic grain** into a hot meal that has none (happens when a liked legume took the side slot) — beside a legume capped at 250 g and only if the meal has 0 other carbs, else ≤2 carb items. Related guards: the hot meal's `hot_side`/`hot_carb` prefers an elastic grain on single-hot days; **a legume side is taken at most once per day** (`LEGUME_SIDE_IDS` check on `used` — legumes are Stage-1 protein levers, not carb levers; a day whose every hot side was a legume measured −33 %); the carb-pile split **skips meals already holding ≥2 carb items**. `crackerMaxN(unitW)` is now **gram-based** (cut/maintain ~36 g, bulk ~54 g, 2–12 pieces) — thin 4 g cakes previously counted like 9 g ones and starved bread slots. Net (4k-profile matrix): restrictive single-hot medians −8.3 % → ≤ −4.1 %, p10 −20…−26 % → −10…−13 %; multi-hot restrictive p10 −24 % → −4 %.

- **Stage 1 — protein → ±7% of `S.proteinG`.** Lever = grams-elastic protein items (`isProt`: meat/fish/legume with no `unitLabel`, not `dip`) **and eggs**. Distributes the protein delta by current-protein share; meats/legumes via `reG`+`clampG`, eggs via `adjustEgg(it, targetG)` (nearest size 15/16/17 = 53/63/73 g × count 1–2). Dairy proteins (cottage/yogurt/cheese) are fixed, so the meat/egg absorbs the delta.
- **Stage 1b — protein top-up injection (once).** When protein is still > 7 % short after Stage 1 (meat at `mainProtCap`, or few sources: vegetarian / kosher+lactose-free), inject **one lean protein portion** — candidates sorted liked-first then protein-per-calorie (cottage/yogurt/tuna-if-unused/egg-if-unused/high-p legume/tofu/supplement) — into the lowest-protein non-hot meal that passes `kosherOk`; sized to the deficit via `pick([f]…)`. Stages 2–3 of the same loop absorb the calories. Lifted vegetarian medians 1.32 → 1.57–1.67 g/kg.
  - ✅ **Gated by `tasteOk` (fixed 06/08/2026).** Before the fix the only filters on the target meal were `!removed`, `type !== 'treat'`, `type !== 'hot'` and `kosherOk` — **nothing asked whether the food belonged on that plate**. Breakfast is always the lowest-protein meal and tuna/seitan win the protein-per-calorie sort, so the injection produced **oats-in-milk + honey + pear + canned tuna**, and (vegetarian) **cornflakes + milk + apple + seitan**. Measured at **4.8 % of menus** (1,440-run matrix). The `MEAL_TEMPLATES` were never at fault — the injection is pushed in *after* the template.
- **Stage 2 — fat → ±8% of `S.fatG`**, via `adjustFat(meals)`. **Raise:** salad oil (`_oilG` 0–15 g) → scale **present nuts** (`reNuts`, 10–40 g) → if no nuts exist, **inject a small nut portion (10–35 g) into the snack**. **Lower:** protein-preserving lean-ify swaps `LEANER = {9→10, 20→21, 24→23}` (tuna-in-oil→water, cottage 5%→3%, yogurt 5%→0%) → shrink oil → present nuts → if still over, **swap the fattest meat/fish for the leanest allowed same-tag protein** (`reG` to keep protein grams). **Liked foods are never swapped/lean-ified** — preferences win, so a liked schnitzel/salmon stays even if fat then runs high. The main fat control, though, is the lean-fat preference in `buildMealBest` (above) — it also keeps fatty items like schnitzel (id 3, `gluten`) out of default menus unless the user likes them. Hard case: liked fatty proteins + no oil to remove → fat stays high (accepted).
- **Stage 3 — calories → ±4% of `S.target`, carbs only.** Distributes the calorie delta across carb levers **only** — grams-elastic `hot_carb`/`grain` (no `unitLabel`, capped by goal-aware `grainCap(f)`, see above) **plus count-levers**: sliced bread (`reBread`, 1–2), rice/corn cakes (`reCracker`, 2–6), and **unit starches** (`reUnit`, 1–3 units of sweet potato/potato/corn — was the main undershoot source when a starch was the day's hot carb). It **never grows protein to fill calories** — protein is owned by Stage 1 (which raises it to target when low) — so protein can't overshoot. If carbs are exhausted, the menu **accepts a small calorie undershoot** rather than inflate protein. The loop early-exits only when calories **and** protein **and** fat are all in band. (To keep carb capacity, the hot meal's `hot_side` is a legume only when the user likes one — otherwise an elastic grain, so there's always something to grow.)

**Post-loop infeasibility resolve** (runs once after the loop, so no oscillation with Stage 1): if calories still **overshoot** (`dCal > target·(1+CAL_TOL)`) — e.g. liked fatty foods or bulky plant protein on a low (cut) target — shrink the grams-elastic protein items (incl. liked, *portion only, never swapped*) proportionally, clamped so daily protein stays ≥ **1.6 g/kg** (`S.proteinG×0.8`; for vegans the target is already 1.6 so no room). If it *still* overshoots, set **`S.menuWarning`** (shown as a banner) telling the user the preferences/target can't be met precisely.

**Post-loop fat trim** (right after, before the undershoot top-up so freed calories become carbs): when daily fat still exceeds **1.3× `S.fatG`** even though calories are on target — the case where the user marked several fat-dense foods as liked, which Stage 2 never lean-swaps — **trim the grams** of the fattest meat/fish items (liked included, *portion only, never swapped*), fattest first, until fat ≤ ~1.15×. Each trim is clamped so it never drops below the item's `_minG`/`mainProtFloor` **and** never pushes daily protein under the 1.6 g/kg floor. The freed calories are refilled with carbs by the top-up. Effect (40-seed avg): **maintain + fatty likes 1.46× → 1.21×** (protein headroom exists); **cut + fatty likes 2.01× → 1.88×** only (protein already on the floor → no room to trim without swapping, so the honest `S.menuWarning` for fat > 1.5× carries it). Lean/no likes unaffected (control stays ×1.00).

Helpers: **`setMacros(it, f, g)`** is the single source of truth for an item's `cal/p/c/fat/fib` (food values are per-100g) — reused by `mkItem` and every lever. `reG`/`reBread`/`reCracker`/`reUnit`/`reNuts`/`adjustEgg` set `g`+`dispG` then call it; `recalcSalad(sg)` rebuilds a salad group from `_comps`/`_oilG`; `recalcMeal(m)` re-sums meal totals. **Truthful unit labels**: any food with `plural`+`unitG` is snapped to whole units by `mkItem` and labeled "N {plural}" ("3 תמרים", "2 בננות בינוניות") — the label always matches `g`. Other natural-portion items are never rescaled. `buildSalad` returns via `recalcSalad` and keeps `_comps`/`_oil`/`_oilG` so the oil stays tunable.

**Measured accuracy** (06/07/2026, 8k-profile matrix: diets × goals × schedules × like-modes, deterministic seeds; 0 crashes/NaN, **0 coherence-invariant violations** — the suite checks kosher, diet/allergy, truthful unit labels, bread/fruit/cracker/cheese caps, ≤2 carb items per hot plate, condiment/drink roles, salad base, tuna caps, variant groups): every segment's **median within ±4.1%**; regular/multi-hot p10 ≥ −5%. **Residual tails**: gluten-free/restrictive single-hot p10 ~−10…−13% (structurally carb-poor: no gluten breads/oats, crackers gram-capped) — now surfaced honestly by a **generic >8% `S.menuWarning`** set in `buildMenu` after reconcile (under- and over-shoot variants). **Inherent limits**: vegan ~1.5 g/kg protein (target 1.6); vegan/vegetarian **cut** can overshoot because the protein floor is untouchable; fat can't drop if every liked protein is fatty.

### 🔬 Diagnosed 07/08/2026 — where the 84 short menus actually come from

Instrumented run (logger inside the Stage 1b block; script kept at `internal/diag-protein.mjs`). **The candidate pool was never empty in any of the 84 — this is not a food-database gap.**

| bucket | n | what happened |
|---|---|---|
| **trigger never fired** | **51** | Stage 1b checked and correctly declined: it saw 129–134 g against a 136 g target (miss 2–7 g, threshold 9.5 g). Protein was then **lost after the check — median 20.3 g, max 27.9 g** |
| injected, still short | 24 | one injection cannot close a ~50 g gap |
| ran, injected nothing | 9 | every (food, meal) pair rejected |

**Root cause of the 51 (the majority):** the **post-loop fat trim** ([app.js:992](../../app.js#L992)) is deliberately allowed to cut protein down to `S.proteinG * 0.8`. For a trained omnivore that is 0.8 × 2 g/kg = **exactly the 1.6 g/kg floor, so it is correct**. But `S.proteinG` is **already** reduced to 1.6 g/kg for vegans and non-trainers, so the multiplier **double-discounts them to 1.28 g/kg**. Vegans are already exempted (`vegan ? 1 : 0.8`) — **the same fix was simply never extended to the other reduced-target profiles.**

**Root cause of the 9:** not the taste gate. Rejections split **405 by `hasProteinAnchor` vs 27 by `tasteOk`**, and removing the taste gate rescues **0 of 9** while removing the anchor gate rescues **9 of 9**. In a vegetarian day every meal is built around dairy, so every meal already has an anchor and nothing can be injected. The guard asks "is there an anchor?" rather than "would this combination read as wrong", so it also blocks cottage-beside-yogurt, which is perfectly ordinary.

⚠️ **The −10% warning threshold above is mis-targeted and should be revisited with these two fixes.** Of the 84 menus it fires on, **63 land at 1.6–1.79 g/kg** (at or above the real floor — nutritionally fine, so this is crying wolf) and only **21 land below 1.6 g/kg** (down to 1.17). All 21 are **vegetarian**: 11 noTrain, 10 morning — precisely the double-discounted profiles.

✅ **Fix 1 shipped 08/08/2026 — correct, safe, and much smaller than expected.** The floor was duplicated at **two** sites (the calorie-overshoot protein shrink and the fat trim), both `S.proteinG * (vegan ? 1 : 0.8)`, and the first even carried the comment `~1.6 ג/ק"ג` — the intent was always 1.6, the multiplier was a shorthand that breaks for reduced targets. Both now call one helper `protCutFloor()` = `min(S.proteinG, 1.6 × S.protRefW)`, with `S.protRefW` set once in `calcMacro` as the single source of truth. **Measured: 24 of 1,440 menus changed, 0 of them omnivore** (the number is mathematically identical there, exactly as predicted), 22 vegetarian, 2 kosher; calorie and fat accuracy unmoved to the decimal.

⚠️ **But it barely dented the shortfall**: menus under 1.6 g/kg went **101 → 100**, >10% shortfalls **84 → 80**, worst case unchanged at −40.4% / 1.17 g/kg. **So in the severe cases the floor was never the binding constraint.** `vegetarian|cut|morning` targets 160 g and lands at 95 g — that is 33 g *below* the floor itself, meaning protein was never cut down to the floor, it never got there. The "lost after the check" story holds for **omnivores** (they land exactly on 1.6 g/kg because the guard works); the severe vegetarian cases are a different mechanism and are **still undiagnosed**.

### ✅ Resolved 08/08/2026 — the defect was **10 menus, not 100**

The whole "sub-1.6 g/kg" framing above compared the engine against **our target instead of against the guideline**, and the target is deliberately aspirational. Checked against the actual recommendations:

| population | guideline | our target |
|---|---|---|
| sedentary, in a deficit | **1.0–1.2 g/kg** | 1.6 |
| training, in a deficit | **1.6–2.2 g/kg** | 2.0 |

RDA is **0.8 g/kg** and is explicitly a *minimum* (unchanged for 70+ years); health bodies publish no "optimum", only the AMDR (10–35% of energy, tolerating up to ~2.8 g/kg). The higher-protein literature converges on **≈2× RDA = 1.6 g/kg** for weight management and lean-mass preservation — i.e. **our 1.6 lands exactly on the evidence-supported figure.** ⚠️ Its *provenance* in the code is still undocumented (`(vegan || noTrain) ? 1.6 : 2` — the non-trainer inherited the vegan branch, whose stated reason was "hard to reach 2 from plants", which does not apply to a non-training omnivore). **The number is right; only its justification was missing. Do not lower it** — the aspirational target also drives satiety, which is what drives adherence.

⇒ Of the 100 sub-1.6 menus, **90 are non-trainers and only 1 of those falls under 1.2 g/kg** — they are inside guideline, not defective. The genuine shortfall is the **10 trained menus** (all `vegetarian` + `morning`), 1.19–1.58 g/kg against a 1.6–2.2 guideline.

**The warning was re-keyed to the guideline floor** ([app.js](../../app.js), search `protLow`): non-trainers warn under **1.0 g/kg**; trained warn under **1.6 g/kg _and_ more than 15% below their own target**. The second condition is required — a flat "trained < 1.6" fires on **141 vegan menus** whose target *is* 1.6 and who land at a median 1.57, i.e. the practical plant-protein maximum. Measured: fires on exactly **10**, leaves **0** genuinely-low menus silent in either group, total warnings 107 → 52, and **all 1,440 menus byte-identical**.

**Still open (small):** those 10 `vegetarian|morning` menus. The mechanism is *not* the taste gate and *not* the cut floor — see the anchor-guard finding above (every vegetarian meal is dairy-anchored, so nothing can be injected). Yellow cheese capped at 4 slices/meal (`maxMeal:60` in data.js); `pick()` re-snaps to whole units after clamping so unit labels never lie (watermelon 250 g "slice" bug).

## Planned treats (`S.treats`)

`S.treats` is an **array** of `TREATS` ids (data.js, ids 200+) — multiple allowed (e.g. coffee + chocolate). In `buildMenu`: the treats' **summed** calories/fat/carbs **and protein** are subtracted from the targets **before** building (floors 800 kcal / 20g fat / 50g carbs / 10g protein — protein was left untouched until 09/11/2026, which gave a shawarma day 185 g against 164 while `rebuildRest` already subtracted it; the post-build protein check adds the treat's protein back and compares against the full target), the menu is built+reconciled against the reduced target, then `S.target` is restored and one standalone `type:'treat'` card holding **all** treat items is appended (never touched by reconcile). Over-target / >25% warnings use the **sum**. **Zero-cal treats** (Coke Zero, `cal:0`): no budget is reduced, warnings stay silent, the note becomes "🥤 על חשבון הבית — בלי השפעה" (`treatBuildNote`), and the workout-window treat-tip is suppressed when the treat card total `cal === 0`. UI: picker overlay (`openTreatPicker`); `chooseTreat` **pushes** to `S.treats` (mid-day add appends to the existing card + `rebalanceDay`; pre-eaten = full rebuild). `removeTreatItem(idx)` drops one treat (frees budget → `rebalanceDay`); `removeTreat` clears all. `S.treats` is restored from the treat card on day reload (`loadDay`). Rebuilding wipes day check-marks behind a confirm.

### Meal-like treats (`type: 'treatmeal'`, 09/11/2026)

A shawarma is eaten **instead of** a meal, not on top of one. Before this, every treat was a timeless side card and all four meals shrank around it (shawarma ⇒ four ~250 kcal meals).

- **Which treats:** `meal: true` in data.js (shawarma 208, falafel 209, burger 207 — 207/208 also `meatTreat` for kosher), and **pizza of 2 or 3 slices** (215/216, `hidden` — reached through "כמה משולשים?" in the treat picker; 1 slice = 206, an add-on). Until 10/11/2026 "the third single slice becomes a meal" was the rule; nobody would have discovered it, so it was dropped. Coffee, drinks, chocolate, ice cream stay add-ons. **At most one per day**; a second meal-like treat is an ordinary add-on (not blocked — the product's tone is "הגזמנו קצת", not a ban).
- **Which meal it replaces:** the user picks (`S.treatFor` = the meal's label; `openTreatSlotPicker` in ui.js, asked only for meal-like treats; mid-day only uneaten/unedited meals are offered). Default (`defaultTreatSlot`): a non-workout meal — the untagged hot lunch **only if the day has another hot meal**, else dinner. ⚠️ On the morning schedule lunch is the only hot meal; replacing it removed the day's only meat and drove 182 runs under 1.6 g/kg.
- **Build:** the slot is spliced out of `mealDefs`, the rest's `pct` renormalised, and if the replaced meal was the **only hot meal** (the user's explicit pick) the last untagged dinner becomes `hot` (without it falafel days fell under 1.6 g/kg 94% of the time). The treat meal is inserted back at the slot's index with its label/time/tag and `origType`. A meat treat joins the kosher 6-hour rule at the slot's time.
- **It is a meal:** ✓ אכלתי, progress count, coach `day_summaries` (planned count unchanged, so no adherence inflation). Locked like a treat (`rebalanceDay` `isLockedIdx`, Stage 1b targets). No edit / alt / add-item; ✕ = `removeMealTreat` (clean day ⇒ rebuild; mid-day ⇒ the meal comes back empty and `rebalanceDay` fills it).
- **Warnings:** the 25% warning counts **add-on** treats only (720 kcal for lunch is not unusual); "הגזמנו קצת" (<800 left) still uses the total.
- **Next day:** `loadDay` returns `{ rebuild: true }` and the app builds a fresh menu — yesterday's base was shaped around the treat (refilling only the slot left no-training days >8% under target 1 in 3). **Restoring a favorite keeps the meal-treat** (Alex, 10/11/2026): the other meals were built around it, and rebuilding the slot produced a ~720 kcal "afternoon snack" that often left the day under target. Small add-on treats are dropped on restore, as before. ✕ on the card removes it.

**Measured** (`internal/harness-menu.mjs <out> 12 treats`, 4,032 runs, same seeds): treat-free menus **byte-identical** (1,440/1,440); shawarma protein **+29% → ~0%**, calories **+5.3% → +1.1%**; shawarma days with a meal under 150 kcal **84 → 47** (control 38); trained days under 1.6 g/kg **0** for every treat; no meat main under its floor; **0 kosher violations** in 1,200 targeted runs. Mid-day accuracy is the existing `rebalanceDay` envelope (falafel: median +5%, p90 +11.6% — the same as an add-on treat).

## Meat on the plate (13/11/2026) — supersedes the 70/85/90 floor quoted above

Found while picking a sample day for the "תפריט תזונה למתאמן" SEO page: only 21 of 3,000 days gave a trainee two real meat mains. Measured on 3,840 menus (`internal/seo/exp/meat-measure.mjs`): **29% had a meat main under 100 g (bulk: 49%)**, 6% a hot meal with no meat at all (12% on noon/evening schedules — on noon always a workout meal), 2% yogurt on a hot plate.

**Why:** the light meals are built first and each brings a whole cup/tub, so together they over-deliver protein — **half the day's protein came from outside the hot meals**. Meat is the only lever Stage 1 can shrink, so it sat on the floor, or the concentration rule removed it. Bulk was worst *because* it has more snacks at the same protein target.

Three changes:
- **Protein steered from the source** (`buildMealBest`): for a non-hot meal, a penalty on protein above the meal's proportional share (`S.proteinG·budget/target`, weight 1.0). Only when the user has meat/fish available (vegetarian/vegan light meals *are* the protein source — 0 menus changed there), and not for `proteinSnack` (`ctx.wantProtein`). Same from-the-source principle as fat and fiber: no reconcile stage.
- **`mainProtFloor()` = min(goal cap 100/110/120, ~5% of the calorie target in grams), never under 70.** The goal-only floor pushed a 1,200-kcal cut with two hot meals to 1,304 kcal (+8.7%) — caught by `engine-review-test`, not by the experiment (that body wasn't in its matrix). 1,200 ⇒ 70 g, 1,600 ⇒ 80 g, 2,000 ⇒ 100 g. A treat day's reduced target lowers the floor too.
- **The cottage/yogurt anchor was removed from `convertDemeatedMeal`.** A de-meated meal is a hot plate; "יוגורט יווני + פסטה מלאה 249g + חציל" is not a meal, and `tasteOk` let it through because dairy is neutral. Egg and legume remain.

**Measured after** (same 3,840): main under 100 g **29% → 6%** (bulk 49% → 0%; the rest are low calorie targets, where the floor is lower by design), hot meal without meat **6% → 2%**, yogurt on a hot plate **2% → 0**, median smallest main 118 → 128 g, protein outside the hot meals 49% → 43%. Harness (same seeds): 398/1,440 treat-free menus changed, **0 vegetarian/vegan**; calorie |dev| median 2.3% / p90 3.8% unchanged; days >8% off 32 → 33; warnings 58 → 59; treat mode essentially unchanged. A floor-only variant was rejected: it inflated protein (>+10% over target in 170 menus vs 92) and added meatless hot meals.

## Per-item removal (`removeItem` / `balanceAfterRemoval`)

Each meal card has a ✏️ **edit toggle** (`toggleMealEdit`, pure CSS `.editing` class; hidden on `type:'treat'` and ✓-eaten meals) that reveals an ✕ on every item row. ✕ = `removeItem(mi, ii)` — **"just skip"**: splices the item, `recalcMeal`, drops it from the daily summary/progress, **no rebuild** (other meals untouched); empty meal → `m.removed = true`. A green note explains it and offers an opt-in **"⚖️ אזן את ההמשך"** button via the transient `DAY.noteAction` field (rendered beside `DAY.note`, not persisted). `balanceAfterRemoval(mi)` reuses `rebuildRest` (locks the edited meal as eaten with its **remaining** items — the user ate the rest — and rebuilds only the untouched meals); if the meal was emptied it falls back to plain `rebalanceDay`. Treat rows instead show an always-visible ✕ → `removeTreatItem`.

## Day correction — `rebuildRest(meals, eaten, mealIdx, actualItem)`

"אכלתי משהו אחר": replaces the reported meal's items with what was actually eaten — **one or more items** (UI cart: TREATS / DB search+grams / `manualItem(name, cal)` — conservative macros: p=0, 60/40 carb/fat), locks all eaten meals (+ a not-yet-eaten planned treat), computes remaining targets, and acts by tier (every tier returns a `note` shown as the green day banner — the full-rebuild tier explicitly says the change is **for today only**):
**Workout meals are protected** (`m.tag` = pre/post): never removed in any tier — muscle preservation beats a small calorie overage. In tiers 2/3 they become a light **protein snack** (`proteinSnack`: snack template, falls back to a direct protein pick — cheese/yogurt/egg/meat/fish/legume — if the template came out low-protein); the tier notes mention the kept workout meal.
- **`tR ≤ 0`** (crossed daily target): open meals removed (workout meals → protein snack), positive banner with the overage ("חצית את היעד... מחר דף חדש"; no punishment meals, no compensating deficit — safety by design).
- **`0 < tR < 300`**: keepers = open workout meals (or one light snack if none) + supportive note; the rest removed.
- **`tR ≥ 300`**: full rebuild of open meals with the existing engine — `buildMealBest` per meal + `reconcile` — against temporarily-swapped `S` targets (restored in `finally`). **Two-way meal-count adaptation**: drops trailing **untagged** meals while `tR/openCount < 260` (workout meals never dropped), adds 1–3 extra snacks when `perMeal > 450/600/800` (cap: 6 open meals). The mid-day treat add/remove flows (`chooseTreat`/`removeTreat` in ui.js) reuse `rebalanceDay` without touching eaten meals.

**Measured** (2000 random disrupted days): 0 crashes/NaN; ~15% hit the over/light tiers; of the fully-rebuilt days ~93% land within ±12% of the original daily target (the residual is structural — e.g., the hot meal was the one replaced).

## Meal Templates (the realism mechanism)

Meals are built from **templates** (`MEAL_TEMPLATES` keyed by `breakfast`/`hot`/`snack`/`dinner`), not free category-mixing — so every meal is a coherent plate by construction.

`buildMeal(type)` → `chooseTemplate()` (keep templates whose required slots are fillable for the diet; prefer ones containing a liked food; weighted-random) → `buildFromTemplate()` fills each slot.

A **slot**: `{ match(f,used) | special, calPct, protPct?, max, optional?, spread? }`.
- `special`: `'salad'`→`buildSalad`; `'hotveg'`/`'hotveg_or_salad'`→`buildSingleVeg`/(~40% hot veg else salad); `'hot_carb'`→prefers an unused carb category via `ctx.usedCarbCats`; `'hot_side'`→one starchy side that is a **legume** (only if liked **and no legume side taken yet today** — see top-up section) **or** a `hot_carb` — never both; `'dip'`→optional hummus/tahini side (~25% / if liked), its own row.
- a template may carry `when(used)` (e.g. the `legume` hot template is feasible only when no meat/fish is available — vegetarians/vegans).
- otherwise `pick()` from `ALL.filter(match)`.
- `spread:'ifAlone'` → `makeSpread()` adds a condiment (tahini/PB) to a bread/cracker **only if the meal has no protein yet** (so cottage/egg/tuna meals get no spread). The bread's `displayName` shows "עם X"; the spread is a **separate row** with its own grams/calories.
- **Pita** (39,40, `pita` flag): dropped from bread pools unless the slot has `pitaOk` (only the eggs breakfast bread slot), and even there only ~30% of builds. All other bread slots use the `_sliced` matcher (sliced bread, no pita).
- **Crackers** (45,46,100, `cracker`) stay valid bread substitutes for everyone, but are **one type per menu** via a `VARIANT_GROUPS` entry `[45,46,100]` (no more "rice cakes large + small + corn cakes" in one day — was ~58% of menus, now 0). For this to stay feasible, **`slotFeasible` checks `variantBlocked`** so a template is never chosen when its only filler is variant-blocked, and the `carb_cheese` snack's carb slot uses `_sliced` (bread/cracker, not cracker-only) so it always fills even when the cracker is blocked — this is what keeps vegan snacks (whose fallback was a lone cracker) from going empty. Net: ≤1 cracker per menu, **0 empty meals** across regular/GF/vegan/vegetarian. The per-meal cracker cap is **gram-based** via `crackerMaxN(unitW)` (cut/maintain ~36 g, bulk ~54 g, 2–12 pieces — a thin 4 g cake is not a 9 g one), used in `crackerPortion`, the Stage-3 `maxOf`, and the top-up counters. **Cracker-count normalization (end of `reconcile`)**: an item that lands at **>6 pieces** is swapped to a larger-unit cracker from the same variant group with the same grams ("12 דקות" → "5 גדולות"); a liked cracker is never swapped for an unliked one, and `used` is updated so the one-type-per-menu rule holds.
- **dairy_fruit** snack pairs fruit only with cottage/yogurt; white/yellow cheese go to `carb_cheese` / cheese-bread (always with a carb), never alone with fruit.
- **fruit_nuts** snack / oats nut slot use `nuts` only (almonds/walnuts/cashews) — not avocado/olives (those are salad extras).
- **yogurt_bowl** topping is **granola only** (cooked oats 41 don't belong in a yogurt bowl); topping is optional so yogurt+fruit still works.

Templates: **breakfast** eggs / cheese / yogurt_bowl / porridge / cornflakes / oats_water / bread_spread (vegan-gated: bread+spread+fruit/nuts, `when` no egg/dairy — covers vegan & vegan+GF whose oats are gluten-excluded); **hot** meat (w3) / legume (w1, veg-only) — **always a cooked meat/fish (or legume) main + carb; no canned tuna here**; **snack** dairy_fruit / fruit_nuts / carb_cheese (bread-or-cracker + optional cheese) / shake; **dinner** cheese_bread / tuna_bread / big_salad (canned tuna lives here).

**`big_salad` protein** = egg/cheese (animal). A legume satisfies it **only when the user has no animal protein available** (`!hasAnimalProtein()` — no allowed egg/meat/fish/dairy), i.e. vegans. For omnivores/vegetarians, beans are never "the protein" of a salad meal (they get egg/cheese); vegan dinners stay feasible via legume/tofu.

## Food role flags (enforce realism, set in `data.js`)

- `condiment` (olive oil, tahini, peanut butter) — never standalone; only via `makeSpread` on bread/cracker
- `drink` (milk) — never a protein; only the `milk` slot in cornflakes template
- `complete` (oatmeal-with-milk 106) — self-contained breakfast; its template has no protein slot
- `dip` (hummus-spread 52, tahini 91) — a side dip in hot meals; excluded from legume main/side pools

Legumes for **omnivores**: only a side in a meat meal (`hot_side`) or in `big_salad` — never a standalone hot main. For **vegetarians/vegans** the `legume` hot template is the main (legume + grain + veg), since no meat is available.

## Taste profile — `tasteProfile(f)` / `tasteOk(f, meal)`

The **only pairwise item-vs-item rule besides `kosherOk`**. `MEAL_TEMPLATES` build coherent meals, but anything injected *after* the template bypassed them entirely (see Stage 1b) — which is how tuna ended up in a porridge.

- **`sweet`** — `sweet_topping` / `granola` / `cereal` tags, plus **oats by id (41, 106)**. ⚠️ The `grain` tag covers bread too, which is neutral, so the classification cannot lean on it.
- **`savory`** — `meat` / `fish` / `tuna` / `veg` / `hot_veg` / `salad` / `salad_only` / `egg`, plus `legume` when not a `dip` (hummus/tahini are spreads, not a legume portion).
- **`neutral`** — everything else: dairy, bread, crackers, nuts, supplements, **and fruit**.

**Rule: sweet and savory never share a meal; neutral goes with anything.**

Two deliberate choices, both load-bearing:
- **Three-way, not binary.** Binary would have blocked **cottage + fruit** (`dairy_fruit`) and **bread + fruit** (`bread_spread`) — combinations the templates already build.
- **Fruit is neutral** — fruit beside a savory meal is perfectly normal. This keeps the `sweet` set tiny, so the rule only fires on meals built around porridge / granola / cereal, which is exactly where the injection was wrong.

**Companion rule — `proteinAnchor(f)` / `hasProteinAnchor(m)`:** Stage 1b also never injects a protein into a meal that **already holds a protein anchor** (egg / tuna / fish / meat / supplement / legume / dairy with `p≥5`; dips, condiments and drinks are not anchors, so hummus on the side and milk on cereal don't count). Without it the same top-up produced **greek yogurt + canned tuna** as an afternoon snack and **white cheese + seitan** — 2.6 % of menus, **none of which existed with the injection disabled**, i.e. entirely self-inflicted. It does not starve the top-up: the templates that carry **no** protein at all (porridge, cornflakes, oats-in-water, bread+spread, the fruit-and-nuts snack) are precisely the meals Stage 1b was built for, and they stay open. Measured: duplicates **31 → 0**, 3.2 % of menus changed (13.9 % of them vegetarian, where protein top-ups are most needed), protein/kg, calorie accuracy and warning counts unchanged. The two rules compose: a sweet porridge now admits only a *neutral* anchor-free candidate (cottage / yogurt / protein powder), never tuna or seitan.

Applied in **Stage 1b** and in **`convertDemeatedMeal`**'s anchor pool. The other post-template injections (nuts→snack in `adjustFat`, the fruit guarantee, the top-up grain) draw from **neutral-only pools by construction**, so a gate there would be dead code.

**Measured (06/08/2026, 1,440-run matrix — 5 diets × 3 goals × 4 schedules × 2 bodies × 12 seeds, seeded PRNG so both runs are comparable):** clash menus **4.8 % → 0 %**; **exactly the 69 clashing menus changed and nothing else** (4.8 % of runs differ, confirming the blast radius); protein g/kg medians **unchanged in every diet segment** (omnivore 1.98, vegetarian 1.91, vegan 1.56, kosher+LF 1.96, GF 2.00); calorie medians and p10 unchanged; `S.menuWarning` counts unchanged. Even within the 69 affected menus the average protein change is **−0.000 g/kg** (worst −0.01) — the engine always found an equally good alternative meal, so the fix cost nothing.

⚠️ **Suite gap this exposed:** the 8k coherence invariants (kosher, diet/allergy, labels, caps, carb-count, condiment/drink roles, salad base, tuna caps, variant groups) had **no "one meal never holds both a sweet and a savory item"** check, so the suite passed on an engine producing tuna-in-porridge. Any harness rebuilt from this spec must include it — and must be confirmed to **fail on the pre-fix code** (it did: 69 hits).

## Tuna rule

`tunaUsed(used)` gates all tuna pools: only one tuna type per menu, capped at one can (`maxDay:160`).

## `topUpMeal()` — completing a trimmed meal (13/08/2026)

Removing an item used to set `edited`, which **locks** the meal, so `rebalanceDay` could never complete it and instead bolted a "נשנוש נוסף" onto the end of the day. `removeItem` now also records `m.trimmed` and `m.trimOut` (`{id, cal, p, tags}` per removed item), and `topUpMeal(meal, used, ctx)` fills the gap.

- **Builds into the existing meal, never from scratch** — same pattern as `convertDemeatedMeal`. The pool is filtered by `tasteOk(f, meal)`, so a candidate must sit with what is still on the plate; `pick()` already enforces `allowed` / `used` / `variantBlocked`, so variant groups and day-level variety come free.
- **The removed food never returns** — that is the whole reason it was removed.
- **Like replaces like**: candidates sharing a tag with what was removed are preferred (spread for spread, starch for starch), because `tasteOk` blocks sweet-vs-savoury but not "a hot dish inside breakfast".
- **At most 2 items, and the second may not share a tag with the first.** Without that rule the fill picked twice from the same family and produced buckwheat *and* pasta on one plate: correct macros, incoherent meal.
- Whatever doesn't fit is left. The day then sits under target and the existing day-level warning handles it; `topUpMeal` never touches another meal.

**Measured (55 runs × 5 profiles incl. vegetarian / kosher / gluten-free):** 0 removed-food returns, 0 taste violations, 0 in-meal duplicates, 0 variant-group duplicates, 44/55 meals back to ≥90% of their original size. **Negative control** with `tasteOk` stubbed to `true` and the family rule removed: 1 taste violation + 3 same-family pairs in 26 runs, so the measurement has teeth (the 06/08 lesson).

## Variant groups (one per menu)

`VARIANT_GROUPS = [[20,21],[15,16,17],[45,46,100]]`, enforced by `variantBlocked(f, used)` inside `pick()` **and in `slotFeasible`**: one cottage type (3%/5%), **one egg dish per menu** (M/L/XL are separate ids, so `used` alone wouldn't block a second omelet), and **one cracker type per menu** (rice-large / rice-small / corn — stops multi-cracker spam). `adjustEgg` additionally **respects a liked egg size** — if the user liked specific size(s), it only resizes within them. Together these also eliminate the old egg-maxDay micro-bug (two meals converging on the same egg id). The `LEANER` swap (20→21) is safe: replaces in place and checks `usedIds`. **`slotFeasible` checks `variantBlocked`** so a template is never selected when its only filler is variant-blocked (would otherwise leave an empty slot/meal).

## Kosher rule

`kosherOk(f, mealTags)` (checked in `buildFromTemplate` on the generic-slot and dip pools): when `kosher` is selected, a meal never mixes `meat` and `dairy` (fish+dairy stays allowed — tuna+cottage is fine). `mealTags` accumulates the tags of items already in the meal. Templates never mix the two anyway — this is an explicit guarantee that survives future templates. The `adjustFat` swaps are same-category (meat→meat, fish→fish, dairy→dairy) so they can't break it.

**6-hour meat→dairy separation** (across meals, not just within one). Halacha requires ~6 h after eating meat before dairy; the reverse (dairy→meat) needs only rinsing, and the schedule gaps (≥4 h) cover it — so only the meat→dairy direction is enforced. A per-meal **`noDairy`** flag is computed in `buildMenu` **after `mealPlan`** (so appended bulk snacks at 10:30/14:30/16:00/21:30 are included): applies only to **kosher + omnivore** (`!vegetarian && !vegan` — vegetarians/vegans have no meat, dairy stays free), where every `type:'hot'` meal is treated as meat (conservative; also covers fish-lunch days at a tiny variety cost). A meal is `noDairy` when its time falls in `(meatTime, meatTime+360min]`. Breakfast is always before lunch ⇒ never `noDairy` ⇒ the morning cottage/yogurt/eggs are preserved. `isDairyKosher(f) = dairy tag || containsMilk` (catches milk-oatmeal 106, whey/casein/protein-bar). Enforcement points, all gated on `ctx.noDairy`/`m.noDairy`: (1) generic pool in `buildFromTemplate` filters out dairy; (2) `slotFeasible`(threaded `ctx`) drops a template whose **required** slot can only be filled by dairy (optional dairy slots like `carb_cheese`'s cheese just come out empty — `if (s.optional) return true` already skips them); (3) `convertDemeatedMeal` omits the cottage/yogurt anchor under kosher (egg/legume only — both parve); (4) Stage 1b protein injection never puts dairy into a `noDairy` meal (picks egg/tuna/legume there, dairy into breakfast/dinner). A subtle `ℹ️` banner (`S.kosherSep` → `DAY.warn.kosherSep`, serialized with the other warnings) explains the separation to the user. **Measured** (8k matrix, kbase HEAD vs knew): **kosherSep violations 887 → 0**, all other coherence invariants stay 0; kosher-segment accuracy essentially unchanged (calorie medians ≤0.3 pp shift, protein/kg identical or ≤0.15 lower and always ≫1.6 floor; `kosher+lactose_free` byte-identical since dairy was already excluded).

**After the build (10/11/2026).** `rebalanceDay` (mid-day treat, "אכלתי משהו אחר") **never passed `noDairy`** — a rebuilt meal could carry cottage an hour after meat (measured: shawarma added mid-day ⇒ 9 dairy meals inside the window in 30 days). It now recomputes the window for every open meal against the day as it stands (`within6hAfterMeat`: "meat" = a `hot` meal, conservatively, or any meal holding meat / a `meatTreat`; fish is not meat), passes it to every rebuild and to `proteinSnack`'s pool, and an added snack with no time is dairy-free if the day has meat. **Dairy meal-treat (pizza, `dairyTreat`)**: the engine can't move a meat meal already eaten, so the "instead of which meal?" picker **notes** (does not block) any slot within 6 h after a meat meal in the menu — "שים/שימי לב: פחות מ-6 שעות אחרי ארוחה בשרית בתפריט" (no hour count: the app knows menu times, not when the user ate). ## `buildSalad` Rules

- Requires ≥ 2 **regular** (`salad` tag, not `salad_only`) vegetables as base
- Optional 3rd veg from `salad_only` pool (lettuce, cabbage, onion) or a 3rd regular veg
- Olive oil (id:86) always added if permitted — **5g (כפית)**, no daily-use restriction
- Parts display (`fmtPart`): each part shows its `unitLabel` (e.g. "חצי פלפל אדום", "עגבנייה בינונית") — no "name (label)" parentheses; pepper labels include the colour so they stay unambiguous
- Salad extra: optionally adds **avocado (87) or olives (93)** as a savory salad component (when liked or ~30%) — that's their home, never paired with fruit

## Portion Helpers

- `eggDisplay(g, unitW, size)` — rounds to 1–2 eggs by weight, appends size label (M/L/XL)
- `crackerPortion(targetG, unitW)` — snaps to 2–6 pieces using the cracker's own `unitG`
- `cottagePortion(targetG)` — 250g full / 125g half container

## `pick()` Priority

1. Foods in `S.liked` → `allowed()` → not in `used` — **shuffled** (Fisher-Yates)
2. All other allowed foods not yet used — **also shuffled** (for menu variety across regenerations)
3. Serving size: calculated from calorie/protein budget, snapped to `unitG`, clamped to `maxDay`/`maxMeal`

Liked foods always outrank non-liked (liked group comes first); both groups are shuffled internally so menus vary while preferences are still honored. `buildSalad` and `buildSingleVeg` shuffle their non-liked pools the same way.

## `mkItem()` Return Shape

```js
{ f, g, dispG, displayName?, cal, p, c, fat, fib }
```
- `displayName` set only for eggs: "חביתה מביצה אחת (L)"; `dispG` is `''`
- All other items: `displayName` is `undefined`; `dispG` holds the portion string
- `fib` — fiber grams for the served portion (`f.fib || 0`); meals sum it to `totFib`, daily total shown in the summary as "X g / יעד ~Y g" against the `DAY.fibG` snapshot ("~" because DB fiber values are approximate; old saved days without `fibG` fall back to number-only)

**Per-meal realism caps** (`mkItem`): bread ≤2 slices (pita 1); **fruit ≤ ~200 g/meal** (clementine→2, banana/apple→1, small dates stay generous) — so a single meal never shows an unrealistic fruit/bread pile even when the calorie budget would allow it.
